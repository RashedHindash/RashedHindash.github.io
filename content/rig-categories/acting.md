Body mechanics asks what a character is doing. Acting asks why. These rigs are
built for the second question, which is why they carry a detailed face and a
capable body together rather than treating the two as separate jobs.

Both share the same control set: squash and stretch with volume retention, a
switchable IK/FK spine, and full facial controls covering the brows, the eyes
and the mouth shapes. Emma adds slider control for the fingers.

They ship as archives. Unpack the folder, then open the scene inside. Each rig
has its own page below with what it teaches and how to practise on it.
