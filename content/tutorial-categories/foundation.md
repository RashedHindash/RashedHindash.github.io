Nine videos, in order, taking someone from opening 3ds Max for the first time to
a finished walk cycle. Each one introduces a single new idea and keeps
everything before it in play, so the sequence matters more than any individual
video in it.

They pair with the [foundational rigs](/rigs/foundational/). Download the rig,
then follow along. Each video has its own page below with what it covers and
what to watch for.
