Body mechanics is where the foundational exercises stop being separate problems
and start arriving together. A jump is a bouncing ball with a skeleton attached
to it. A lift is weight transfer with a second mass involved. A climb is
contact, released and re-established, over and over. Nothing here is new, but
all of it is simultaneous, and that is a different discipline from doing any one
of it well.

What changes at this stage is the level of control the rig gives you. The
foundational set was deliberately impoverished so that a student could not hide.
These rigs are the opposite: they have enough controls to solve almost anything,
which means the constraint moves from what the rig can do to whether you know
what you are asking it for.

Both rigs here are free. Open the scene and the controls are ready.

They pair with the [body mechanics tutorials](/tutorials/body-mechanics/), which
work through retiming and cleanup on this kind of shot.
