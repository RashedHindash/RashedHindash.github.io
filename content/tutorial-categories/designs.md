Design is the stage where a production decides what it is going to be, and it is
the stage least often shown. Finished concept art circulates widely; the hours
of decision-making that produced it almost never do.

These four recordings are that process, captured whole and played back at speed.
Two are character design sheets, deliberately placed at opposite ends of the
stylisation dial so the same problem can be seen solved two ways. One is a rapid
vehicle thumbnailing pass, which is about generating and discarding ideas
quickly rather than finishing any of them. The last is an environment taken from
morning to night, which is where lighting, mood and set design get settled before
a single asset is built.

There is no commentary on any of them. That is the trade: you see every decision
in the order it was actually made, including the reversals, but nothing explains
itself. The written page accompanying each video is where the explanation lives.
