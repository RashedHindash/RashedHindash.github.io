Longer, less tidy, closer to how the work actually goes. These are recorded
sessions rather than scripted lessons, so expect to see the mistakes and the
fixes.

Two series so far. **Turn** works through retiming and cleaning up a turn, from
the pelvis outward to the head. **Sit** covers a sitting animation. Both are
about revising existing animation rather than starting from nothing, which is
most of what animation actually is and almost none of what gets taught.
